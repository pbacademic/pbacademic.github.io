01 · Profile

About

I received my PhD from the University of Calcutta in 2014. My doctoral work examined how frequency-domain correlation filters could improve face recognition under varying illumination.

I previously served as Professor and Head of Electrical Engineering at Future Institute of Engineering and Management.

03 · Projects

Research

Applied computer vision and student project mentoring.

Funded project · Principal investigator

Traffic detection and vehicle counting

Used machine learning to count vehicles by category on different road types in Kolkata and Howrah. The project was supported by the West Bengal Pollution Control Board and TERI.

Student project · Mentor

Real-time activity detection from a quadcopter

Guided a student team on a prototype for aerial activity detection and classification. The project reached the finals of DRDO’s Defence Research Unmanned System Exposition in 2018.
